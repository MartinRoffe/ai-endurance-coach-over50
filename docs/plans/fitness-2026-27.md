## Session library

**Threshold / VO2.** From 25 Oct, weekday hard work is the MaxiClimber. Threshold is 5×4 min at RPE 7 with 2 min easy, paced by RPE and heart rate (Karvonen Z4 about 180–191). VO2 is shorter reps, about 6×2 min at RPE 8. A long camp session keeps those short reps and uses easy zone 2 for the time that is left. Easy weekday aerobic work is zone 2 on the MaxiClimber. MaxiClimber stays calendar-only.

**Kettlebell.** Swings, goblet squats, presses, rows, carries, get-ups, and step-ups. Bells are 8, 12, 14, 16, 20 and 24 kg. The heaviest bell is not the deadlift.

**Pull-ups.** Extra line on Tue, Thu, and Sat. Negatives 3×3–5 from 27 Oct (the bar is in, strict reps are still 0). Band-assisted 3×4–6 in January. Strict, or stay on the band, 3×3–5 in February.

**Sandbag.** After Tuesday's hard MaxiClimber session from 4 Jan. Sets leave 2 reps in reserve. A barbell is fine if you have one. There is no trap bar. The test standard is still 102 kg × 15 in one minute, which is not the January load.

**Hill ruck.** The Saturday pack stays at 10 kg. Time is the progression (shorter every 4th week, and on the Saturday before the first 3-hour ride). 13 Feb is the timed 3-mile rehearsal at 20.4 kg.

**Weekend outdoor rides (Z2 Easy · outdoor, Long Ride (Easy) · outdoor).** Pushed to Garmin as cycling workouts. Flat quiet loop, heart rate leads on climbs, Assioma pedals still record power. Lights in the dark months. Wind or weather: the MaxiClimber for the same duration at zone 2. There is no indoor bike.

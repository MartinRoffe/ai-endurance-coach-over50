# How the app works

A short tour of the whole product — from your morning check-in through coach
chat — so you can see how the pieces fit together. Use this as the starting
point; the other Help pages go deeper on each tab.

## What this app is

The AI Endurance Coach pulls overnight wellness and training data from **Garmin
Connect**, stores it locally in SQLite, and turns it into:

- A **readiness score** against your own 30-day baseline
- A live **web dashboard** and optional **daily email**
- Structured **training-plan** sessions on your calendar (and on Garmin)
- An **AI coach** that already knows your form, plan, and recent sessions

The CLI (`endurance-coach`) and the web dashboard share the same data layer.
Fetch once; everything else reads from the local database.

## A day in the life

1. **Overnight** — your watch syncs HRV, sleep, and body battery to Garmin.
2. **Morning** — open **Readiness** (or wait for the scheduled email). Check the
   score, metric tiles, nutrition card, fatigue alerts, and the HRV traffic
   light.
3. **Decide** — green means train as planned. Amber or red may offer an easier
   session; click **Apply** if you want the swap. FTP re-test or power-baseline
   cards use the same Apply button.
4. **Train** — complete the session (structured workouts appear on Garmin when
   you've synced the plan).
5. **After** — open **Analysis** for the AI review of that workout; log RPE and
   in-ride fuelling if you want the coach to remember how it felt.
6. **Ask** — open **Coach** for pacing, fuelling, or “should I change Thursday?”
   questions. Plan changes appear as confirmation cards — nothing mutates until
   you approve.

```text
Watch sync → Fetch / email → Readiness decision → Ride
                                              ↓
                         Coach chat ← Analysis (RPE / fuelling)
                                              ↓
                              Apply plan change → Calendar + Garmin
```

## How a plan change works

Three places can propose a one-day override, but they all share one funnel:

| Source | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **HRV traffic light** (Readiness) | Amber/red suggests an easier variant (or recovery spin) |
| **FTP / power cards** (Readiness) | Schedules a ramp or re-test into a suitable slot |
| **Coach chat** | Proposes a duration or type/label swap after you ask |

When you click **Apply**:

1. The change is saved locally as a **plan override** (source of truth).
2. The app best-effort **pushes that single date** to Garmin Connect
   (unschedules that day's plan workouts, schedules the new one). Shared
   templates on other dates are left alone.
3. The [Calendar](tabs/plan.md) and the daily email show the overridden session.

That surgical push is different from a full **Sync workouts** / `--workouts`
re-upload (delete-and-recreate the whole plan range). Use the full sync when FTP
changes and %FTP templates need refreshing, or after bulk plan edits.

## Coach chat

The coach rebuilds a fresh snapshot of your data on **every message** — readiness,
training load, remaining plan sessions, recent analyses, RPE, fuelling logs,
nutrition, body composition, overrides, and a durable memory memo.

**What it can do:**

- Answer grounded questions about pacing, fuelling, recovery, and your week
- **Propose** a plan change (you confirm on a card)
- Save **commitments** and **session notes** without a confirmation card
- Pull deeper detail on demand (sleep history, a past analysis, meal cycle, etc.)

**What it will not do on its own:** send email, edit Garmin workout structure
directly, or apply a plan override without your approval.

See [Coach](tabs/coach.md) for day-to-day use, and [Memory](tabs/coach.md#memory)
for the durable memo across sessions. Technical readers: [AI Architecture](ai-architecture.md)
and [Coach chat walkthrough](coach-chat-walkthrough.md).

## Where the numbers come from

```text
Garmin Connect  →  fetch  →  local SQLite  →  dashboard / email / coach
```

- **Wellness & activities** arrive via the Garmin API into `history.db`.
- **Readiness** is a composite of z-scores vs your 30-day baseline.
- **AI text** (advice, analysis, coach replies) is generated by Claude when an
  Anthropic API key is set, and often cached so it does not re-bill every reload.

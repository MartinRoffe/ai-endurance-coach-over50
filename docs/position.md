## What to do

| Block | Dose | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| Mobility | 3×/week · ~8–10 min | Hamstring hinge, hip flexors, thoracic extension, pec stretches |
| Strength | On the plan's strength days · ~20 min | Lebe Stark “Perfect 20-Minute Workout For Men Over 40” follow-along (2 RIR) |
| Trunk & shoulder | After every strength session · ~12 min | Side plank, bird-dog / suitcase, wall slides, face pulls — ride days stay just rides |
| Single-leg | Part of the session · ~8 min | Split squat, step-down, single-leg glute bridge |

Buy a **32 kg** bell in September for suitcase carries from October. Keep both strength days through taper and camp weeks — stay well clear of failure.

## Progress markers

Retest **monthly** (first Friday) and write the numbers down:

1. **Bar hover** (on the bike) — endurance pace, hands off the hoods for 3 s without lurching
2. **Front plank** — honest neutral-spine hold (target 90 s)
3. **Seated pike** — cold, no warm-up. Pass = hands past ankles with a flat back. Six-month project; first honest retest late September.

Phases Aug→Dec are on the programme page. Meaningful strength change takes months; trunk endurance often shows on the bike by weeks 3–4.

## What to do

| Block | Dose | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| Mobility | 3×/week · ~8–10 min | Hamstring hinge, hip flexors, thoracic extension, pec stretches |
| Strength | 2× week · ~20 min | Lebe Stark “Perfect 20-Minute Workout For Men Over 40” follow-along (2 RIR) |
| Trunk & shoulder | After Strength 1 & 2 · ~12 min | Side plank, bird-dog / suitcase, wall slides, face pulls — ride days stay just rides |
| Single-leg (optional) | After Strength 1 · ~8 min | Split squat, step-down, single-leg glute bridge |

Buy a **32 kg** bell in September for suitcase carries from October. Keep both strength days through taper and camp weeks — stay well clear of failure.

## Progress markers

Retest **monthly** (first Friday) and write the numbers down:

1. **Pike push-up** — quality reps to form failure
2. **Front plank** — honest neutral-spine hold (target 90 s)
3. **Bar hover** (when on the bike) — endurance pace, hands off the hoods for 3 s without lurching

Phases Aug→Dec are on the programme page. Meaningful strength change takes months; trunk endurance often shows on the bike by weeks 3–4.

## Fit changes (parallel)

One change per ride-week — do not stack them. See the programme page for the fit sequence and red flags that mean stop adjusting and get a physio assessment.

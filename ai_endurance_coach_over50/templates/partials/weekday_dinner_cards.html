W1 · Mon · BBQ rub chicken breast + AF potatoes + corn + vinegar slaw
Monday~20 min finishServes 166g P620 kcal
Ingredients (1 plate)
  • 180 g chicken breast fillet
  • 1 tsp mild BBQ rub (no chilli) · garlic-infused oil · salt
  • 200 g potato, cut for air fryer
  • 80 g sweetcorn (tinned, drained) · vinegar slaw (¼ white cabbage, splash cider vinegar)
  • Dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Sunday: portion chicken; toss in rub + oil; bag. Chop potato; store fridge. Shred cabbage for slaw.
  2. Night: air-fry potato 15–18 min at 200°C, shaking once. Air-fry chicken 12–14 min until 75°C+.
  3. Warm corn; dress slaw with vinegar + salt (crisp, not swimming).
  4. Plate protein + ~150 g potato + corn + slaw. Dessert after. Optional: finish chicken on Blackstone.
Plate + dessert: 620 kcal · 66g P · 42g C
Where it fits: Cycle week 1 · Monday. No cheese, no spice. Sauce = glaze or spoon only. Crisp veg.
W1 · Tue · Pork loin apple-mustard glaze + mash + green beans
Tuesday~20 minServes 164g P620 kcal
Ingredients (1 plate)
  • 160 g pork loin medallion
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard · 1 tbsp unsweetened apple sauce · garlic-infused oil
  • 180 g potato for mash · splash milk
  • 100 g green beans
  • Dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Sunday: portion pork; mix mustard + apple for glaze tub. Peel/chunk potato.
  2. Night: boil potato 12–15 min; mash with splash milk. Steam or microwave beans crisp-tender.
  3. Pan or air-fry pork 8–10 min; spoon glaze over last minute (thin coat — not a sauce pool).
  4. Plate pork + mash + beans. Dessert after.
Plate + dessert: 620 kcal · 64g P · 45g C
Where it fits: Cycle week 1 · Tuesday. No cheese, no spice. Sauce = glaze or spoon only. Crisp veg.
W1 · Wed · Soy-ginger chicken stir-fry + basmati + crisp peppers
Wednesday~15 minServes 165g P620 kcal
Ingredients (1 plate)
  • 180 g chicken breast, thin strips
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce · grated ginger · spring-onion greens · garlic-infused oil
  • 90 g dry basmati (or ~180 g cooked Sunday batch)
  • 150 g mixed peppers + sugar snaps (or frozen stir-fry veg)
  • Dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Sunday: strip and bag chicken with soy + ginger. Cook extra basmati for this night if not using lunch batch.
  2. Night: hot wok/pan, oil, chicken 3–4 min; add veg 2–3 min — keep crunch. Splash soy.
  3. Serve over rice. Dessert after. No chilli.
Plate + dessert: 620 kcal · 65g P · 48g C
Where it fits: Cycle week 1 · Wednesday. No cheese, no spice. Sauce = glaze or spoon only. Crisp veg.

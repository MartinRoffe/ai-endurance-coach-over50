Sunday cook list — Cycle week 1
  • Lunch: Herb thighs (#01) · Chinese Wed (#01b) · Basmati (#02)
  • Breakfast: Overnight oats (#05) · Scotch eggs (#07) · Yogurt pots (#04)
  • Dinner A (fridge Mon/Tue): Chicken breast & root-veg traybake
  • Dinner B (freeze → Wed/Thu): Beef & red-lentil ragù
OrderTaskWhy now
1 · 0:00Oven 200°C. Tray herb chicken (#01) + Chinese Wed (#01b) + Dinner A traybake (breast fillets + root veg + potatoes).Longest cooks share the oven.
2 · 0:05Start batch basmati (#02).Passive simmer.
2b · 0:10Hob: brown mince for Dinner B ragù; soft veg; lentils + passata; simmer 25–30 min.Hands-off while breakfasts assemble.
3 · 0:152 scotch eggs (#07) + 3 oats jars (#05).See #16.
4 · 0:25Yogurt pots (#04); move next protein-bar freezer pack if using #03.5-min job.
5 · 0:40Portion chicken + rice tubs; cool traybake → Batch A fridge ×4.Cool before sealing.
6 · 1:00Freeze Sat/Sun chicken; Batch B ragù → freezer ×4.Wed/Thu never sit as day-4 fridge meat.
WeeknightsTue eve: fry Wed egg fried rice; move Batch B to fridge. Reheat dinners piping hot + dessert.
Friday eveRice cakes — count from calculator.~30 min separate.

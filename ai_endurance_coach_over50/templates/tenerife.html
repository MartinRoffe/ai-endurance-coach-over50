Teide Ascents

Camp Philosophy

Recent weeks run ~90–125 km / 5–7 h of bike. This camp is still a clear volume jump (~315 km week 1, ~335 km week 2), structured so the only true epic is Day 9 Teide out-and-back. Easy days protect legs; Masca is deferred until W/kg supports the 14% valley ramp (2027–28 target). Grounded on FTP 214 W — climbing days at 60–65% FTP (128–139 W).

Legs Openers — Coastal Loop South

Start gently. Head south from Los Gigantes along the TF-47 coast road through Puerto de Santiago and Playa de San Juan, down through Alcalá and on to Callao Salvaje and Playa Paraíso before turning back. Keep the effort conversational — this is about moving your legs after travel, not training. Enjoy the La Gomera views across the channel. Keep heart rate in Zone 1–2 throughout.

02
Medium · Endurance

Tamaimo Climb + Teno Loop

65km

Distance

1,100m

Elevation

▶
☕ Coffee at Bar Soto, Santiago del Teide

Your first proper climb. Head north out of Los Gigantes on the TF-454 toward Tamaimo — 5.8km at 7% average, a proper Category 2 effort. Reach Santiago del Teide for coffee, then swing northwest into the Teno foothills toward Erjos and El Tanque, staying up on the plateau before retracing the descent through Santiago del Teide and Tamaimo back to Los Gigantes. Steady endurance pace — this establishes your aerobic base for the week.

Route

Los Gigantes → Tamaimo (TF-454) → Santiago del Teide → Erjos → El Tanque → back via Erjos → Santiago del Teide → descend Tamaimo → Los Gigantes

Key Climb

Tamaimo: 5.8km / 7% avg — Cat 2. Steady effort, keep something in reserve.

Effort

Zone 2–3. Climbs at solid tempo, descents and flats at easy endurance.

Notes

First real climb — don't smash it. Bring a light jacket for the descent.

03
Medium · Recon

Chío Recon — TF-38 Lower Slopes

55km

Distance

950m

Elevation

▶
☕ Coffee at Bar Parada, Chío — turnaround café

Recon only — not the summit. Head north through Tamaimo and Santiago del Teide onto the TF-38 lower slopes, stop at Bar Parada in Chío, then descend the same way home. About 3.5–4 hours. The job today is arriving at Day 9 intact: learn the road, check bottles/refills, keep power honest. Teide waits for the queen stage.

Route

Los Gigantes → Tamaimo → Santiago del Teide → Chío (Bar Parada) → descend same route

Why not summit

Days 1–3 stay under ~10 h cumulative. Summiting on day three of camp, in August heat, is the quiet accumulation risk — not a badge.

Effort

Cap 120–130 W (140 ceiling). Zone 2 feel. Save 128–139 W climbing for Day 9.

Notes

Practice the Chío refill. Note how cold the first kilometres of descent feel — kit check for Day 9.

Easy · Recovery

Active Recovery — Harbour Spin

30km

Distance

300m

Elevation

▶
☕ Coffee at harbour café, Los Gigantes — linger as long as you like

Yesterday was the Chío recon. Today is about flushing your legs and protecting your form for mid-week. Ride south along the coast through Playa San Juan and Alcalá to Callao Salvaje and back, keeping everything Zone 1. No climbs, no effort, no heroics. This is where amateurs make mistakes by going too hard — trust the process.

Route

Los Gigantes → Puerto de Santiago → Playa San Juan → Alcalá → Callao Salvaje → return. Flat as possible.

Effort

Zone 1 only. If you can't hold a conversation, you're going too hard.

Recovery Protocol

Post-ride: legs up for 20 mins. Sea swim if possible. Foam roll quads & calves.

Optional

Skip the bike and swim/walk instead. Real recovery sometimes means no saddle.

05
Medium · North Coast

Tamaimo + North Coast — No Masca

80km

Distance

1,400m

Elevation

▶
☕ Coffee at Buenavista del Norte — town square café

North coast scenery without the Masca valley. Climb Tamaimo to Santiago del Teide, stay on the plateau through Erjos toward El Tanque and Buenavista del Norte for coffee, then return the same high roads. Do not descend into Masca — at 214 W / 2.38 W/kg the 14.8% ramp out is ~3 km/h / ~25 rpm on a 34×36; that is walking territory, not grit. Save the mountain for Day 9.

Route

Los Gigantes → Tamaimo → Santiago del Teide → Erjos → El Tanque → Buenavista → return via El Tanque → Erjos → Santiago → Los Gigantes. Skip Mirador de Masca.

Key Climb

Tamaimo: 5.8km / 7% avg — Cat 2. Steady endurance only; no 11–15% ramps.

Effort

Cap 120–130 W (140 ceiling). Arrive at Day 9 intact — that is the only job.

Notes

Start early (7–7:30am). Masca is a 2027–28 project (~3.4 W/kg). Bigger cassette (36/40) still helps every steep you do ride.

Full Rest — Explore Los Gigantes

0km

Rest

Protocol

▶

A full rest day between the two training blocks is non-negotiable. You've covered roughly 315km and 4,700m of climbing in 6 days — your body needs this. Walk to the cliffs viewpoint, take the boat trip to see the 600m vertical walls from the sea, or just eat, sleep, and stretch. Stay off the bike completely. This is where the adaptation happens.

Morning

Sleep in. Gentle walk to the cliffs viewpoint or along the harbour promenade.

Activity

Optional: boat trip to see Los Gigantes cliffs from the sea. 30 min swim. Light stretching.

Nutrition

Eat well but slightly less volume — you're not burning much today. Focus on quality protein and carbs to repair.

Evening

Plan week 2 mentally. Visualise the Teide out-and-back. Sleep 9 hours. Lay out gels for 80–90 g/h.

09
Hard · Queen Stage

Teide O&B — Chío to Cable Car

115km

Distance

2,400m

Elevation

▶
☕ Chío (~35 km) + Parador near the top — then reverse

The bucket-list climb: Playa de la Arena / Los Gigantes → Chío → Boca Tauce → cable car, then descend the way you came. Roughly 115 km / 2,400 m, mean gradient ~6%, about a 7-hour day at 128–139 W (60–65% of FTP 214 W). Sea level to 2,363 m in one climb — no south-side loop, no Masca, no 14.8% ramps. Hard, memorable, at the edge of current fitness rather than past it.

Route

Los Gigantes / Playa de la Arena → Tamaimo → Chío → TF-38 → Boca Tauce → cable car → descend same road home

Key Stats

~2,400 m gain, mean ~6%. Moving ~7 h. Longest recent ride was ~5.4 h — this is a stretch, not a fantasy.

Effort

128–139 W on the climb (60–65% FTP). Start 6:30 — August west coast; a 7am start puts you descending in peak heat.

Fuelling

80–90 g/h from roll-out. Chío refill ~35 km in; Parador near the top. Carry more than you think — out-and-back means same bottles home.

Tamaimo + Coastal Finale

95km

Distance

1,500m

Elevation

▶
☕ Coffee at Santiago del Teide — deserved after Day 9

Satisfying camp finale without Masca or a second Teide. Climb Tamaimo, roll the plateau toward Santiago / Erjos, then swing south along coastal and lower roads for length without the gradients that punish tired knees two days after the queen stage. Accumulated fatigue barely costs duration — it savages 140 W on a 12% ramp. So there are none today.

Route

Effort

Downgrade

Legs dead or heat high → cut to 60–70 km coastal only. Surviving Day 9 intact already means the camp worked.

Pro Camp Nutrition

Eat Like You Mean It

At this volume, nutrition is training. Under-eating is the most common mistake at cycling camps. The targets below are for hard days — adjust down by 15–20% on easy and rest days.

Hard Day Calories

3,200–3,800

kcal total. More for 100km+ days with big elevation.

Carbohydrate Target

8–10g/kg

Body weight per day. Carbs are your fuel — don't fear them.

Protein Target

1.8–2.2g/kg

Per day for muscle repair. Spread across all meals.

On-Bike Fuelling

60–90g carbs per hour while riding. Gels, bars, rice cakes, bananas. Start at 30 mins in — don't wait.

Hydration

500–750ml per hour on the bike. Add electrolyte tabs — you're sweating in Tenerife's heat. 2–3L total on big days.

Recovery Window

Within 30 mins of finishing: 60–80g carbs + 20–30g protein. Chocolate milk, rice + eggs, or recovery shake.

Easy Day & Rest Day Adjustment

On easy days, reduce overall calorie intake by ~15–20% and cut back on simple carbs. Prioritise protein and vegetables. On the full rest day, eat intuitively — your hunger will drop naturally. Don't under-eat protein on rest days: that's when repair happens.

The fitness gains from a camp emerge 2–4 weeks after you return, as your body adapts. The first week home may feel flat — this is normal and expected.

Pre-camp · 5–12 Aug

Fitness is fixed — safety is gearing, gut, heat, pacing

Nothing before Aug 13 changes FTP. Use the remaining days for the things that actually move camp risk.

Heat acclimation

Highest value still available. 5–10 days is the classic window — hit 6 of the next 8: overdressed Z2 on the Pinnacle, or 20 min sauna post-ride. August west coast rewards this more than any extra intensity.

Gut training

80–90 g/h must be routine, not novel. Use remaining long rides with the exact products you'll carry on Teide day.

Gearing & cadence (physics)

At 100 kg system / 140 W: 12% → ~4.1 km/h → 34 rpm on 34×36 (not 46). On 14.8% Masca ramps ≈ 28 rpm / walk territory — why Masca is out of this camp. 36t is the official 105 Di2 RD limit; 40t needs GRX RD-RX820 or a hanger extender.

FTP check

Model hangs on 214 W (logged 22 Jul 2026). If you've been consistent since and believe it's stale, re-test — but do not invent watts. Masca returns around ~3.4 W/kg / ~275 W.

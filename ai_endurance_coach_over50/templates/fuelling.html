What to take
Before: one banana, about 120 g, 30–60 minutes before a hard or long session.
During: plain rice cakes, about 25 g each, eaten across the session with water. Rides of about 2 hours and up also take maltodextrin mixed into a bottle so the hour hits the carb target.
After: 250–300 ml semi-skimmed milk within an hour. That is the recovery drink.
Easy and rest days do not add a ride snack on top of the one toast-and-honey snack the plate already allows.
By ride length

Rice cakes cover the solid carbs. From 2 hours 30 minutes, maltodextrin bottles close the gap to the gut-training target. Electrolyte bottles are the drink beside that, not instead of it.

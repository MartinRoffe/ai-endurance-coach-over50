BBQ / BLACKSTONE

Evening dinners are now air-fryer/oven plates every night. Use the Blackstone only when you fancy finishing a mild BBQ-rub protein outdoors — never as the required Fri–Sun path.

When to use it

  • Cycle weeks with BBQ chicken / BBQ pork on the evening plates menu
  • Same macros as the card — protein + carb side + veg/slaw + skyr dessert
  • No chilli, no cheese, no saucy stew finishes

Quick rules

  • Preheat; oil lightly; cook to 75°C+ in the thickest part
  • Finish veg crisp on a cooler zone — don’t stew them in sauce

11Weekday Batch Dinners

Four-week rotation. Build weeks (1–3) plate at 620 kcal / 62–68 g protein (smaller carb side + 150 g protein dessert). Recovery week (4) is lighter at 560 kcal. Cook the full batch for 2 people × 2 nights; plate the carb amount on each card so macros match nutrition_plan.WEEKDAY_DINNERS and Today.

Batch Logic
Read firstNo spice
Storage map
  • Batch A — fridge Sunday; eat Mon + Tue (day 1–2)
  • Batch B — freeze Sunday; move to fridge Tue eve; eat Wed + Thu
  • Portions — cook for 2 people × 2 nights = 4 adult portions per batch
  • His macros — protein share + smaller carb side + protein dessert (see each card)
  • Fri–Sun — Blackstone griddle (unchanged)
Rules
  1. No chilli, no hot sauce, no spicy pastes — mild korma-style only where curry appears.
  2. Reheat piping hot (steaming throughout). Rice dishes: cool fast, fridge promptly, reheat once only.
  3. Label containers: week + batch + date. Freezer bags work for Batch B stews/ragùs.
  4. Protein dessert after every Mon–Thu dinner — 150 g skyr or 0% Greek yogurt.
  5. Active-week cook sheet and checklist: Sunday Prep.

Easy Protein Boosters
Hit the floor
Standard (every Mon–Thu)
  • 150 g skyr or 0% Greek yogurt — +90 kcal, +15 g P (already in dinner macros)
If the day is short
  • Cottage cheese pot (~200 g) — +~34 g P
  • Half-scoop whey stirred into yogurt — +~12 g P
When to use
  1. Protein dessert is non-negotiable on weekday dinners — it is the easy lever to 60–68 g at the slot.
  2. Reach for cottage cheese or whey-in-yogurt when Garmin / the checklist shows you will miss the daily floor.
  3. Do not cut the dessert on recovery week — protein floor holds; calories come from the lighter main.
Reheat & Food Safety
Storage
Rules
  • Reheat until steaming hot throughout
  • Batch A: fridge ≤2 days (Mon/Tue)
  • Batch B: freeze Sunday; thaw in fridge from Tue eve
  • W3 A rice: cool fast, reheat once only
Why freeze Batch B
  1. Cooked meat left in the fridge from Sunday to Thursday is day 4 — outside the comfortable window.
  2. All four Batch B recipes freeze well (ragù, bolognese, stew, casserole).
  3. Tuesday night: move Wed+Thu portions to the fridge so they thaw safely.
Rice (W3 A): cool within 90 minutes, shallow containers, fridge. Reheat once to piping hot. Never reheat rice twice.

11Weekday Batch Dinners

Four-week rotation. Build weeks (1–3) land ~700–740 kcal / 62–68 g protein per portion. Recovery week (4) is lighter at ~560 kcal. Protein dessert (150 g skyr or 0% Greek yogurt: +90 kcal, +15 g P) is standard on every weekday dinner — do not skip it.

Storage map

  • Portions — cook for 2 people × 2 nights = 4 adult portions per batch

Rules
  1. No chilli, no hot sauce, no spicy pastes — mild korma-style only where curry appears.
  2. Reheat piping hot (steaming throughout). Rice dishes: cool fast, fridge promptly, reheat once only.
  3. Label containers: week + batch + date. Freezer bags work for Batch B stews/ragùs.
  4. Protein dessert after every Mon–Thu dinner — 150 g skyr or 0% Greek yogurt.
W1 A · Chicken & Root-Veg Traybake + Roast Potatoes
Batch A~50 min4 portions (2×2)66g P700 kcal
Ingredients (2 people × 2 nights)
  • 700 g chicken breast fillets (boneless, skinless)
  • 800 g potatoes, cut into ~3 cm chunks
  • 600 g mixed root veg (carrot, parsnip, onion), chunked
  • 3 tbsp olive oil · 1 tsp dried thyme · 2 garlic cloves, crushed · salt + pepper
  • Per serving dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Heat oven to 200°C fan. Line a large baking tray (or two if crowded — veg needs space to crisp).
  2. Pat chicken dry with kitchen paper. Toss potatoes and root veg with 2 tbsp oil, thyme, salt and pepper. Spread in a single layer.
  3. Rub chicken with the remaining oil, crushed garlic, salt and pepper. Nestle the fillets among the veg — not stacked.
  4. Roast 25–35 min until potatoes are golden and chicken is cooked through (no pink, juices run clear, 75°C+ in the thickest part). If veg needs longer, lift the chicken onto a plate and give the tray 5–10 min more.
  5. Rest chicken 5 min, then slice or leave whole. Divide into 4 equal portions in fridge containers (2 people × Mon/Tue). His plate target: ~½ of one night’s chicken + ~250 g roast veg/potato.
  6. Cool within 1 hour, lid on, fridge. Reheat Mon/Tue until piping hot; add the protein dessert after.
His portion + dessert: 700 kcal · 66g P · 52g C
Where it fits: Week 1 Mon/Tue. Shares the 200°C oven with Sunday lunch chicken (#01). Breast fillets cook faster than bone-in — use the thermometer, not the old 45–50 min timing.
W1 B · Beef 5% & Red-Lentil Ragù + Basmati
Batch B~45 min4 portions64g P730 kcal
Ingredients (2 people × 2 nights)
  • 800 g 5% beef mince
  • 200 g dried red lentils (rinsed)
  • 1 large onion + 2 garlic cloves + 2 carrots, diced small
  • 700 g passata · 1 stock cube · oregano · 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tbsp olive oil · salt + pepper
  • 400 g dry basmati (cook fresh each night, or use Sunday batch rice)
  • Per serving dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Rinse the red lentils in a sieve under cold water until the water runs clearer.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large deep frying pan or casserole on medium-high. Add the mince; break it up with a wooden spoon. Cook 6–8 min until browned (no pink clumps). Tip into a bowl if the pan is crowded; return when veg is soft.
  3. Lower to medium. Soften onion, carrot and garlic 5–7 min until the onion looks translucent, not browned.
  4. Stir mince back in with lentils, passata, crumbled stock cube, oregano, bay, salt and pepper. Add a splash of water if it looks very thick. Bring to a gentle bubble.
  5. Simmer uncovered 25–30 min, stirring now and then, until lentils are soft (no hard centre) and the sauce is thick. Fish out the bay leaf.
  6. Cool slightly, then divide into 4 freezer tubs. Freeze Sunday. Tuesday evening: move 2 tubs to the fridge for Wed/Thu.
  7. Eat nights: reheat ragù until piping hot; cook or reheat basmati; plate; protein dessert after.
His portion + dessert: 730 kcal · 64g P · 84g C
Where it fits: Week 1 Wed/Thu. Freezes well — do not leave in the fridge from Sunday to Thursday.
W2 A · Mild Coconut Chicken Curry + Basmati
Batch A~40 min4 portions65g P720 kcal
Ingredients (2 people × 2 nights)
  • 1 kg chicken thigh fillets, diced into bite-size pieces
  • 1 onion, sliced · 2 garlic cloves, crushed · thumb ginger, grated
  • 3–4 tbsp mild korma paste (check label — no chilli)
  • 1 × 400 ml light coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp oil · large handful spinach · 400 g dry basmati
  • Per serving dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Heat oil in a large saucepan on medium. Soften the onion 5–6 min until translucent (not brown).
  2. Add garlic, ginger and korma paste. Stir 1 min until it smells fragrant and coats the onion.
  3. Add the diced chicken; stir to coat. Cook 4–5 min until the outside turns from pink to white.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk; scrape the bottom of the pan. Bring to a gentle simmer (small bubbles, not a hard boil).
  5. Simmer ~20 min, stirring occasionally, until chicken is cooked through (cut the largest piece — no pink). Wilt the spinach in for the last 2 min.
  6. Cook basmati (or use Sunday batch). Divide curry + rice into 4 fridge containers (Mon/Tue × 2 people). Cool, fridge, reheat piping hot + dessert.
His portion + dessert: 720 kcal · 65g P · 58g C
Where it fits: Week 2 Mon/Tue. Korma-style only — check the paste label for heat.
W2 B · Beef 5% Bolognese + Wholewheat Spaghetti
Batch B~45 min4 portions68g P700 kcal
Ingredients (2 people × 2 nights)
  • 800 g 5% beef mince
  • 1 onion + 2 garlic cloves + 2 carrots + 1 celery stick, all diced small
  • 700 g passata · oregano · 1 bay leaf · salt + pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 400 g wholewheat spaghetti (cook night-of)
  • Per serving dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Heat oil in a large deep pan on medium-high. Brown the beef mince 6–8 min, breaking it up, until no pink remains.
  2. Lower heat to medium. Add onion, carrot, celery and garlic. Soften 6–8 min until the onion is translucent and the carrot is starting to soften.
  3. Stir in passata, oregano, bay, salt and pepper. Bring to a gentle bubble, then simmer uncovered 25–30 min until thick and rich. Stir every few minutes so it doesn’t catch. Remove bay leaf.
  4. Cool slightly; divide sauce into 4 freezer tubs. Freeze Sunday. Tuesday evening: move 2 tubs to the fridge.
  5. Wed/Thu: reheat sauce until piping hot. Boil spaghetti in salted water per pack (usually 10–12 min) until just tender; drain. Plate sauce over pasta; protein dessert after.
His portion + dessert: 700 kcal · 68g P · 62g C
Highest protein of the build-week dinners — useful when the day is running short. Proper beef bolognese with 5% mince keeps fat in check.
W3 A · One-Pot Chicken & Chorizo Rice (Mild)
Batch A~45 min4 portions65g P740 kcal
Ingredients (2 people × 2 nights)
  • 800 g chicken thigh fillets, cut into large chunks
  • 150 g mild cooking chorizo, sliced
  • 1 onion, diced · 1 pepper, diced · 2 garlic cloves
  • 400 g dry basmati · ~800 ml hot chicken stock (enough to cover by ~1 cm)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika (sweet, not hot) · peas optional · 1 tbsp oil
  • Per serving dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Heat oil in a wide lidded pan on medium-high. Brown chicken chunks 4–5 min; add chorizo 2–3 min until it releases orange fat. Move meat to a plate.
  2. Soft onion and pepper 5 min in the same pan; add garlic and paprika 1 min.
  3. Stir in the dry rice so every grain is coated. Return chicken and chorizo. Pour in hot stock to cover by about 1 cm; stir once.
  4. Bring to a simmer, lid on, medium-low. Cook ~18 min without lifting the lid often. Rice should be tender and liquid absorbed. Rest lid-on 5 min. Stir through peas if using.
  5. Cool fast: spread into shallow containers within 90 min. Fridge. Reheat once only until steaming hot.
  6. Divide into 4 Mon/Tue portions; protein dessert after reheating.
His portion + dessert: 740 kcal · 65g P · 60g C
Rice safety: cool quickly, fridge promptly, reheat once to steaming hot. Do not leave cooked rice at room temp.
W3 B · Smoky Bean & Beef Stew + Crusty Bread
Batch B~50 min4 portions62g P710 kcal
Ingredients (2 people × 2 nights)
  • 700 g 5% beef mince
  • 2 × 400 g mixed beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 onion + 1 carrot + 1 celery stick, diced · 2 garlic cloves
  • 500 g passata · 1 tsp smoked paprika (sweet) · 1 stock cube · splash of water
  • 1 tbsp oil · crusty bread to serve (not mash — keeps shopping simple)
  • Per serving dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Heat oil in a large casserole or deep pan on medium-high. Brown the mince 6–8 min until no pink remains.
  2. Add onion, carrot, celery and garlic; soften 5–7 min.
  3. Stir in beans, passata, paprika, crumbled stock cube and a splash of water. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Simmer uncovered 30–35 min, stirring occasionally, until thick and the carrot is tender. Taste; add salt if needed. No chilli.
  5. Cool; divide into 4 freezer tubs. Freeze Sunday; fridge Tuesday evening for Wed/Thu.
  6. Reheat piping hot; serve with crusty bread; protein dessert after.
His portion + dessert: 710 kcal · 62g P · 66g C
Where it fits: Week 3 Wed/Thu. Bread side — no extra mash potatoes to shop for.
W4 A · Lean Cottage Pie + Half-Cauli Mash
Batch A · Recovery~55 min4 portions62g P560 kcal
Ingredients (2 people × 2 nights)
  • 700 g 5% beef mince
  • 1 onion + 1 carrot + 1 celery stick, diced · 2 garlic cloves
  • 400 g passata (or stock + 1 tbsp tomato purée for a lighter gravy)
  • 500 g potato + 500 g cauliflower florets · splash of milk · salt + pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Per serving dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Heat oven to 200°C fan if baking assembled pies. Boil potato chunks and cauliflower in salted water ~15 min until both are soft when pierced.
  2. Meanwhile, brown mince in oil 6–8 min; soft onion, carrot, celery and garlic 5 min. Stir in passata; simmer 10 min until thick. Season.
  3. Drain veg well. Mash with a splash of milk, salt and pepper until fairly smooth (a few cauliflower lumps are fine).
  4. Divide mince into 4 ovenproof dishes or one large dish marked into quarters. Spread mash on top; rough the surface with a fork.
  5. Bake 20–25 min until the top is lightly golden and the filling is bubbling at the edges. Or fridge the assembled portions and bake/reheat Mon/Tue until bubbling.
  6. Cool leftovers promptly. Protein dessert after eating.
His portion + dessert: 560 kcal · 62g P · 40g C
Where it fits: Week 4 Mon/Tue — matches the 2050 kcal recovery tier.
W4 B · Chicken & Veg Casserole + Small Potatoes
Batch B · Recovery~50 min4 portions60g P560 kcal
Ingredients (2 people × 2 nights)
  • 900 g chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 onion + 1 leek + 1 carrot + 1 celery stick, sliced · 2 garlic cloves
  • ~600 ml chicken stock · thyme · 1 bay leaf
  • 600 g small potatoes, halved if large · 1 tbsp oil
  • Per serving dessert: 150 g skyr / 0% Greek yogurt
Method
  1. Heat oil in a large casserole on medium-high. Brown chicken lightly 3–4 min per side (it will finish cooking in the liquid). Move to a plate.
  2. Soft onion, leek, carrot, celery and garlic 5–7 min in the same pan.
  3. Return chicken; add potatoes, stock, thyme and bay. Liquid should almost cover — add a splash of water if needed. Bring to a simmer.
  4. Lid on, medium-low 30–35 min (or oven 180°C fan, same time) until chicken is cooked through (75°C+) and potatoes are tender when pierced. Remove bay leaf.
  5. Cool; divide into 4 freezer tubs. Freeze Sunday; fridge Tuesday evening.
  6. Wed/Thu: reheat until piping hot; protein dessert after.
His portion + dessert: 560 kcal · 60g P · 40g C
Easy Protein Boosters
Hit the floor
Standard (every Mon–Thu)
  • 150 g skyr or 0% Greek yogurt — +90 kcal, +15 g P (already in dinner macros)
If the day is short
  • Cottage cheese pot (~200 g) — +~34 g P
  • Half-scoop whey stirred into yogurt — +~12 g P
When to use
  1. Protein dessert is non-negotiable on weekday dinners — it is the easy lever to 60–68 g at the slot.
  2. Reach for cottage cheese or whey-in-yogurt when Garmin / the checklist shows you will miss the daily floor.
  3. Do not cut the dessert on recovery week — protein floor holds; calories come from the lighter main.
Reheat & Food Safety
Storage
Rules
  • Reheat until steaming hot throughout
  • Batch A: fridge ≤2 days (Mon/Tue)
  • Batch B: freeze Sunday; thaw in fridge from Tue eve
  • W3 A rice: cool fast, reheat once only
Why freeze Batch B
  1. Cooked meat left in the fridge from Sunday to Thursday is day 4 — outside the comfortable window.
  2. All four Batch B recipes freeze well (ragù, bolognese, stew, casserole).
  3. Tuesday night: move Wed+Thu portions to the fridge so they thaw safely.
Rice (W3 A): cool within 90 minutes, shallow containers, fridge. Reheat once to piping hot. Never reheat rice twice.

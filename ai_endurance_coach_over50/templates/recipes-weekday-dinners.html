Mon–Sun evenings

11Evening Plates

Four-week rotation. Build weeks plate at ~620 kcal / ≥60 g protein (carb side + protein dessert). Recovery week ~560 kcal Mon–Fri. Serves 1. Macros match nutrition_plan.EVENING_DINNERS.

How it works
Read firstNo spice · no cheese
Prep map
  • Sunday — marinate/portion proteins for 1; batch rice/potatoes; chop veg & slaw
  • Weeknights — 15–20 min air-fryer / oven / hob finish from the bag
  • Unique nights — no reheated stew twice; crisp plates only
  • Macros — protein share + plated carb side + 150 g skyr dessert
  • Blackstone — optional finish for BBQ rub nights only
Rules
  1. No chilli, no hot sauce, no cheese.
  2. Sauce = glaze or spoon — never a stew bowl.
  3. Veg stay crisp (high roast / brief stir-fry / AF).
  4. Protein dessert after every evening plate.
  5. Active-week sheets: Sunday Prep.

Easy Protein Boosters
Hit the floor
Standard (every evening)
  • 150 g skyr or 0% Greek yogurt — +90 kcal, +15 g P (already in dinner macros)
If the day is short
  • Half-scoop whey stirred into yogurt — +~12 g P (prefer over cheese)
When to use
  1. Protein dessert is non-negotiable — easy lever to ≥60 g at the slot.
  2. Whey-in-yogurt if the checklist shows you will miss the daily floor.
  3. Do not cut the dessert on recovery week — protein floor holds.
Storage & Food Safety
Prep
Fridge map
  • Raw marinated proteins: use within 2–3 days; freeze Thu–Sun bags if prepping Sunday
  • Cooked rice: cool fast, fridge ≤3 days, reheat once piping hot
  • Fish: buy/thaw for the eat day
Finish nights
  1. Air-fryer / oven to 75°C+ in the thickest protein.
  2. Label bags: weekday + protein. Cook for 1 — no giant reheated leftover pots.

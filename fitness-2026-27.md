# General fitness · 12 Oct 2026 – 3 Oct 2027

Road cycling and mountain walking for the trips in this year. Haute Route Alpes 2028 stays the later goal. This file is the same week table the app reads from `ai_endurance_coach_over50/fitness_plan.py`.

## Rules

- Procedure about **Thu 15 Oct 2026**. The week of 12 Oct is easy walking plus an optional flat or turbo ride of 30–45 minutes, only if discharge advice allows exertion and you are pain-free. No pack, no hills under load, no hard strength.
- If you pass fragments, get colic, or are told to wait, repeat the current week and let every later date slide. Stop a session for flank pain, fever, or blood in the urine. Keep drinking around training.
- Once training is allowed: **Mon rest, Tue harder, Wed easy, Thu easy support, Fri rest, Sat on foot, Sun long easy ride.** Two harder days only, with 48–72 hours between them. A missed harder day moves. It does not stack.
- Morning check (HRV, resting heart rate, body battery): if two of three are off, that harder day becomes the easy version or rest.
- If a harder day starts with legs still tired from the last one, repeat the current week’s numbers. On Saturdays, add minutes or pack weight, never both.
- On foot every week. The bike does not train descents or a pack.
- This year has no sweetspot, VO2, or race-sim block. The May 2027 Tenerife race-sim stays off.

## Tenerife cap

## Session library

**Strength — easy circuit** (return, and once in camp). 1 round, stop well short of failure: get-up, push-up, split squat, step-up, one-arm row, calf raise.

**Strength — heavy** (from 4 Jan). Loaded step-up, rear-foot-elevated split squat, single-leg deadlift, slow 4-second step-downs, row or pull-up. Stop with reps in reserve.

**Strength — light.** Same movements, one easy set. Used on Tue 23 Feb, in the light week between the BUSAR test and the Lakes weekend.

**Z2 Easy.** Turbo or flat road. Conversational. Heart rate stays in the easy band you already use.

**Long Ride (Easy).** The Sunday ride, and the longer Tenerife days. Fuel from the first hour on rides over 90 minutes. Flat and easy the day after a weighted Saturday.

**Easy hill walk / Saturday hill walk.** On foot, climbs taken slowly, pack empty or very light until January.

**Hill ruck.** From January. Carry the kilograms in the week table. Add weight or time, never both.

**Keswick days.** See the route pair above. Times are the long option. Start lower on the hill if the wind or the surface says so.

**BUSAR test.** Sat 20 and Sun 21 Feb. Pack test, step-overs, trap-bar deadlift, and burpee pull-ups. Not pushed to Garmin.
